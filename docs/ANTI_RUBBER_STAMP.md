# Anti-rubber-stamp safeguards

An oracle packet must make informed disagreement possible. The packet therefore
records material alternatives, evidence gaps, framing, and an explicit
disposition affordance. `approve` is valid only when alternatives are disclosed
and no unresolved material evidence gap is silently suppressed. `reject` and
`clarify` are first-class decisions, not exceptional failures.

The checker is intentionally synthetic and does not measure human decision
quality. It rejects plausible-looking packets that hide a material alternative
or remove the oracle's ability to reject or request clarification.
