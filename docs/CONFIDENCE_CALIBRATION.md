# Confidence calibration dimensions

AWG records applicability, expected outcome, and downstream-impact confidence
as separate dimensions. A disagreement is meaningful: high applicability does
not imply a safe outcome, and a likely outcome does not remove downstream
risk. Thresholds in this initial contract are explicitly marked
`unvalidated`; empirical calibration is a later evidence task.

The checker rejects records that provide only a collapsed score or that claim
validated thresholds without evidence.
