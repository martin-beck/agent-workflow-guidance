# Evidence for future oracle-free autonomy

Fewer oracle interventions are not a success criterion by themselves. A later
task may proceed without a new oracle decision only when prior history is
scoped to the same task class and context, confidence calibration and outcome
evidence are present, and an independent safety review passes. Corrections and
failures remain part of the evidence record.

Eligibility expires when scope or context changes. The synthetic checker
includes a low-intervention unsafe case to ensure that intervention frequency
cannot bypass outcome and safety evidence.
